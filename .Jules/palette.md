## 2024-05-24 - Checkbox label wrappers
**Learning:** Wrapping a checkbox input in a `<label>` element is a simple and effective way to increase its clickable area, making it much easier for users to select, especially on mobile devices or for users with motor impairments.
**Action:** Always wrap checkboxes and radio buttons in `<label>` elements, either implicitly (by wrapping the input) or explicitly (using the `for` attribute).
